Privacy Policy

EventControl · Last updated 27 September 2026

EventControl (eventcontrol.onrender.com) is a tool for running presentations and screens at live events. This page explains what information it collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address when you request access, and the sign-in details managed by our login provider (Supabase Auth). Your password is handled by Supabase and is never stored in EventControl's own database.
  • Your content: events, files you upload or import (presentations, PDFs, images, videos), the running order, notes and settings you create.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and the IP address of requests, used for security and rate limiting (for example, limiting sign-in attempts). We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, EventControl asks only for the drive.file permission: it can open only the files you choose in Google's own file picker, never the rest of your Drive. It also receives your basic Google profile (name and email) to show which account is connected.

  • Files you pick are copied into your EventControl account so they can be shown at your event.
  • Access tokens from Google are stored encrypted on our server and are used only to import the files you picked.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as needed to run the service.
  • You can disconnect at any time in EventControl's Settings; this revokes EventControl's access at Google. You can also remove access from your Google Account permissions page.

EventControl's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we use information

Only to provide EventControl: signing you in, storing and showing your events and files, sending account emails (such as a verification code or an approval notice), and keeping the service secure. Your events and files are private to your account.

Who processes it

  • Render hosts the website and server.
  • Supabase provides sign-in, the database and file storage.
  • Brevo sends account emails.
  • Google, only if you connect Google Drive.

We do not sell personal information.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your account exists. When an account is removed, its login, events and files are deleted. Demo sessions are deleted automatically after about 10 minutes. To have your account and data deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email us.
